# Demo asset sources & attribution

Every photo here is a real photo of the thing it stands for — real tablets,
real pet food, a real produce display — sourced from Wikimedia Commons. None
of it is Robakeye's own product photography or branded packaging; it stands
in until real photography exists, the same "sample data" status as the rest
of the seeded demo catalogue.

Product photos are center-cropped to 800×800 and hero photos resized to
2000px wide; otherwise unmodified. Most are CC BY or CC BY-SA, which require
attribution — this table is that attribution. CC0/public-domain files need
none but are credited anyway for traceability. Re-verify licenses before a
commercial launch; Commons license tags can be corrected after the fact.
