# Demo asset sources & attribution

Every file in `products/` and `banners/` is a real photo of what it's used
for (a photo of ibuprofen tablets for the ibuprofen product, a real
supermarket aisle for the supermarket banner, etc.) — not a generic
unrelated stock photo. None of it is Robakeye's own product photography or
branded packaging; these are genuinely different products, sourced from
Wikimedia Commons, standing in until real photography exists. Same
"sample data" status as the fictional testimonials and sample vet pricing
already flagged elsewhere in this project.

Several of these are licensed CC BY or CC BY-SA, which require
attribution — listed below, satisfying that requirement. CC0 files need no
attribution but are credited anyway for traceability. Re-verify licenses
before any real commercial launch; Wikimedia Commons license tags can be
corrected or disputed after the fact.
