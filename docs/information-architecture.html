02

Location-aware inventory

Every product page answers one question first: can I actually get this, and from where. Branch match happens once per session and is re-usable everywhere the catalogue is shown.

flowchart TD
    A["Customer lands on any catalogue page"] --> B{"Branch already known this session?"}
    B -- "yes, saved address or\nprevious branch pick" --> F["Catalogue filters to that branch"]
    B -- "no" --> C["Step 1 — Locate\nGPS prompt / manual area entry / pick a saved address"]
    C --> D["Step 2 — Match\nGeofence against the 3 branch coordinates,\nrank by distance and delivery radius"]
    D --> E{"At least one branch\nwithin radius?"}
    E -- "no" --> N["Show: delivery not yet available here\noffer pickup from nearest branch instead"]
    E -- "yes" --> F
    F --> G{"Item in stock at\nnearest matched branch?"}
    G -- "yes" --> H["Step 3 — PDP shows:\nAvailable at [Branch] — X km away"]
    G -- "no" --> I{"In stock at another\nbranch in radius?"}
    I -- "yes" --> J["Step 4 — PDP shows:\nAvailable at [Other Branch]\nmay add delivery time / fee"]
    I -- "no" --> K["Step 5 — PDP shows:\nOut of stock everywhere\nNotify me — email / SMS capture"]

States on the product page

robakeye.com/supermarket/groceries/rice-5kg
Product image
Long-grain rice, 5kg sample
① Available at Branch A — Ajadi · 2.4 km away
Add to basket
② Also sold at Branch C (Sheu, 7.1 km) and Branch B (Ojoo, 9.8 km) — switch branch
    • 1Primary availability line. Always names the matched branch and distance, styled as a positive/confirmed state. This line is the one piece of inventory truth repeated on every card, PDP and cart line.
    • 2Branch switcher. Secondary, collapsed by default. Lets a customer deliberately shop a farther branch — e.g. picking up in person — without that becoming their default match.

Out of stock at the matched branch step 4

① Out of stock at Branch A — Ajadi (your branch)
② Available at Branch C — Sheu · 7.1 km away — may add delivery time or fee
③ Add from Branch C
Notify me at Branch A instead
    • 1Named failure state, not a generic "unavailable" — tells the customer exactly which branch and why.
    • 2The fallback is explicit about the trade-off before the customer commits.
    • 3Accepting this adds the item to a second branch group in the cart — triggers the split-fulfilment flow in section 05.

Fully unavailable step 5

① Out of stock at every branch
② Notify me when available — [ email or phone field ]
Notify me

② writes to a per-product, per-branch waitlist. When inventory marks the item back in stock at a branch the customer subscribed through, fire the SMS/email and clear the subscription — a customer never gets notified twice for the same restock.

Edge case — cart spans two branches

A customer adds rice from Branch A, then later adds dog food only available at Branch C (the flagship, since it's the only one with a vet/pet aisle). The cart does not block this; it groups it. Checkout then treats it as two sub-orders under one payment — detailed in section 05.

RuleBehaviour
Branch match is stickyStored for the session (and on the account, once logged in) so it isn't re-run on every page. A manual "switch branch" always overrides it.
Radius is per-branch, not uniformBranch C (flagship) may justify a larger delivery radius than A and B. Store radius as a per-branch setting in admin, not a hardcoded constant.
Stock check is real-timeThe "in stock" read happens at page load and again at add-to-cart, not cached — two customers racing for the last unit at a branch must not both succeed.
Pickup bypasses matchingIf the customer chooses in-store pickup instead of delivery, any branch with stock is offered, regardless of radius.
03

Prescription medication flow

Nothing tagged prescription-only becomes purchasable without a named pharmacist's approval, and every approval is logged against a licence number.

sequenceDiagram
    participant C as Customer
    participant W as Web app
    participant P as Pharmacist (admin)
    participant S as SMS / Email

    C->>W: Opens a Prescription Required product
    W-->>C: "Add to cart" replaced by "Consult Pharmacist to Order"
    C->>W: Taps CTA
    W-->>C: Opens consult modal (chat / WhatsApp / callback form)
    C->>W: Submits name, phone, medication, preferred contact time
    W->>P: New request enters Review Queue
    P->>P: Reviews request, prescription upload (if any), patient history
    alt Approved
        P->>W: Approves, enters licence ID
        W->>W: Generates one-time unlock token (time-limited)
        W->>S: Sends unlock link via SMS/email
        S-->>C: Customer receives link
        C->>W: Opens link — item becomes purchasable for this customer
        C->>W: Completes purchase within the token's validity window
    else Rejected
        P->>W: Rejects, enters a reason
        W->>S: Sends rejection notice + suggested next step (e.g. "visit a branch")
    end
    W->>W: Logs event: requester, pharmacist, licence ID, decision, timestamp

Pet profile, confirmation & the post-visit record

Pet profile fields

  • Name, species, breed, sex, date of birth or estimated age
  • Known conditions / allergies (free text)
  • Linked owner account (one account, multiple pets)
  • Photo (optional)

Post-visit record

  • Visit date, service type, attending vet
  • Clinical notes (vet-entered, customer-visible summary)
  • Medication prescribed — links into the prescription flow if it's a controlled item
  • Next visit due date — surfaces as a reminder on the account Overview (section 07)

Cart & order management

One calculation order, applied the same way whether the cart has one item or three branches' worth.

Calculation order

04Loyalty points redeemedCustomer chooses how many points to spend−₦500

05Delivery feeBy distance and provider — see section 06. Two sub-orders = two fees, see below.+₦1,800

Discounts stack in the fixed order above — combo discounts never recalculate after a promo code is applied, and loyalty points are the last reduction before the subtotal locks. This order must be enforced server-side at order creation, not just reflected in the UI, so the price a customer sees at review is the price they're charged. All amounts above are sample figures.

Section-by-section notes

Prescriptions

  • History of consult requests with status (section 03), not just approved orders.
  • Re-upload or re-consult directly from an expired entry.

08

Assumptions & open items

Decisions this spec made in order to stay concrete, that the business should confirm before design or build locks them in.

  1. Branch A and B's vertical mixAssumed Pharmacy + Supermarket, no vet clinic, since only the flagship is described as full-service. Confirm, since it changes the branch-match and vet-redirect logic directly.
  2. Geofence radiiPer-branch delivery radius and in-house-rider radius are both treated as admin-configurable settings here, with no real values supplied. Needs real numbers per branch before section 02 can be built.
  3. Delivery partner listGokada, Kwik and Fez are named in the brief as examples. Confirm which are actually under commercial agreement, and get each one's quote/booking API shape before building the adapter interface in section 06.
  4. Tax treatmentNigeria commonly zero-rates essential medicines for VAT; supermarket goods typically don't get the same treatment. The calculation order in section 05 assumes per-category tax codes exist — confirm which categories are taxed and at what rate.
  5. Prescription token validity windowModelled as time-limited in section 03 without a stated duration. Needs a business decision (24–72h is a common range) and should be checked against PCN record-keeping and dispensing guidance.
  6. Loyalty points — earn and redemption rulesSection 05 shows points being redeemed; earn rate, expiry, and whether points differ by vertical are undefined here.
  7. Alcoholic beverages age gateFlagged in the sitemap as gated at cart. Confirm whether Nigerian regulation requires ID capture/verification at checkout or delivery, not just a checkbox.
  8. Split-order refunds and partial cancellationNot covered in section 05. If one sub-order fails (stock pulled after payment, courier can't fulfil), the refund/replace flow for just that sub-order needs its own spec before build.
