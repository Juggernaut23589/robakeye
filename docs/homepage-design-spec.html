Design system basics

The numbers every section below is built from. Colour and type tokens are inherited unchanged from the brand guide — this section adds what the homepage needs on top: grid, spacing scale, breakpoints, motion.

  • Content max-width 1200px, centred
  • Side margin 24px minimum, grows to fill above 1440px

Tablet · 768px

  • 8 columns, 20px gutter
  • Side margin 24px
  • Mega-menus collapse to accordions

  • 4 columns, 16px gutter
  • Side margin 16px

Component padding uses space-3/4 (12–16px). Gaps between cards in a row use space-4/6 (16–24px). Space between homepage sections uses space-16/24 (64–96px) on desktop, halved to space-8/12 (32–48px) on mobile.

Motion

TokenValueUsed for
--dur-fast150msIcon/colour micro-transitions (nav link underline, focus ring).
--dur220msDefault — button and card hover, shadow, scale.
--dur-slow300msMega-menu open/close, hero slide crossfade.
--easecubic-bezier(.3,0,.2,1)Standard ease-out — snappy start, soft landing. Applied to all of the above.

Every transition in this spec respects prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — durations collapse to near-zero rather than being removed, so layout still updates, just without the animated transition.

Mobile nav

Layout

  • Hamburger left, logo centred, cart + account right — matches the brief exactly.
  • Search collapses to an icon that expands to a full-width overlay on tap.

Menu drawer

  • Slides in from the left, 85% viewport width.
  • Pharmacy / Veterinary / Supermarket / Our Locations as accordions, not mega-menus.
  • Location selector pinned above the accordion list.

Behaviour

  • Navbar hides on scroll-down, reappears on scroll-up — reclaims vertical space on a small screen.
  • Announcement bar is dismissible and the dismissal is remembered for the session.

Accessibility checklist

RequirementApplied as
Colour contrastAll text/background pairs reuse the ratios verified in the brand guide (section 01) — nothing on this page introduces a new pairing.
Keyboard navigationEvery interactive element (nav links, mega-menu items, carousel arrows, quick-add) is reachable by Tab in visual order; mega-menus open on focus, not just hover.
ARIAHero slider: role="region" aria-roledescription="carousel", live region announces slide changes only when paused. Quick-add: aria-label="Add [product] to basket".
Alt textEvery product, branch and hero image needs a written description at build time — no image ships without one.
MotionAuto-advancing carousel stops under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce; shimmer/skeleton animations shorten to near-zero rather than disappearing.
10

Assumptions & open items

Calls made to keep this spec concrete, for the business to confirm before Figma or build locks them in.

  1. Store hoursShown as a 7am–9pm sample across all three branches. Needs real, possibly branch-specific hours — the flagship's vet clinic may keep different hours from its pharmacy/supermarket floor.
  2. App download badgesSpec shows "coming soon" placeholders since no app exists yet per the IA spec's scope (web + admin only). Confirm whether these should be removed entirely for v1 rather than teased.
  3. Promo countdown sourceThe combo banner's timer needs a real end-time from the Promotions & Loyalty admin module (IA spec, section 01) — not a hardcoded value in the frontend.
  4. Real photographyEvery background described as "pharmacist image," "pet + owner photo," "produce imagery" etc. is a placeholder gradient here — these need the actual shoot from the brand guide's photography brief (section 06) before high-fidelity Figma.
  5. Testimonial sourcingThree sample quotes are placeholder layout content only. Replace with real, consented customer reviews — and decide how "Verified purchase" is determined (linked order ID, most likely) before launch.
  6. Payment & social logosRepresented as neutral text chips here to avoid reproducing trademarked artwork. Source the providers' actual logo files and usage guidelines for the Figma handoff.
